01Japanese sake

Sake, studied closely and shared generously.

Sake is where my curiosity about science, craft and food meets.

I took the WSET Level 3 Award in Sake, passing with Distinction, and was one of the two highest-scoring students worldwide, which led to my being awarded the Dassai Sake Prize, including a bursary to visit the Dassai brewery in Yamaguchi, Japan. I’ve since taught sake classes and led tastings in the UK.

03About

A physicist turned game developer, with a serious interest in sake.

I’m Luther, based in Leeds. I studied Physics at the University of Oxford, specialising in quantum computing, then spent nearly three years as a full-stack software engineer at The Phoenix Partnership, a medical software company.

In late 2023 I left to focus on games. After a games development bootcamp at The Developer Academy in 2024, I now work as an indie game developer and a freelance 3D modeller for product design.

Alongside that, Japanese sake has become a serious pursuit. I hold the WSET Level 3 Award in Sake, won the Dassai Sake Prize, and have taught sake classes and led tastings in the UK. More on the sake page.
