Offbeat

A rhythm game in which several people at a party talk to you at once, and you have to keep up with all of them. It reflects what navigating social situations can feel like when you’re neurodivergent.

Made in a single weekend by a team of three for the Global Game Jam 2026. I was the programmer and wrote it all in C++ in Unreal Engine 5.
