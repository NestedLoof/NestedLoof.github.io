## Must NEVER be claimed or implied

- That he **runs an import/distribution business**. The sake page says explicitly that he doesn't currently, but is very interested in talking to Japanese breweries, UK importers/distributors, bars and restaurants.
- That he **works for, represents or is affiliated with Dassai, WSET or any brewery**. The Recognition panel states he doesn't work for or represent Dassai; keep that note.
- That he has **visited breweries he hasn't**, or **taught events he hasn't**. Only list classes/tastings that happened.
- Any **invented testimonials, photos, events, client names or numbers**.
- A **named commercial game as inspiration** (Hive Mind was described as inspired by a specific PS1 game; this was changed to "classic puzzle games" to avoid legal issues). Don't reintroduce it, or similar names/logos.
- Anything from the **WSET results letter or certificate** (candidate numbers; WSET asks that certificates not be reproduced), or the **Dassai polishing card** (Dassai promotional material).

- Short sentences. No slogans, no marketing adjectives, no filler. See the **AI-look list in [design.md](design.md#avoid-the-ai-look)**; Luther actively removes anything that sounds generated.
