# Design

How the site looks and why. Everything here was decided with Luther across the 2026 redesign; the rationale is in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Concept: "polished to the core"

Sake rice is milled until only the starchy heart remains (the *seimai-buai* polishing ratio). The site follows the same idea: restrained, precise, nothing decorative that doesn't earn its place. The look is **Japanese editorial**: warm paper, ink-black type, a muted vermilion, hairline rules, generous space. It is deliberately not a template look, not a dashboard, not "tech portfolio".

It has to hold two personalities without splitting in two: a **games developer** and a **sake educator**. The solution is one shared system (type, grid, spacing, components) with a **colour theme per section**:

| Section | Theme | Accent |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero, About | Paper (`--paper`) | Vermilion |
| Sake band on the homepage | Near-black (`--night`) | Vermilion (light) |
| Games band on the homepage, whole `/games/` page | Deep indigo `#18202D` + faint low-poly wireframe | Rice-straw `--koji` |
| `/sake/` page | Paper, with tinted panels | Vermilion |
| Contact | Light on the homepage; indigo `--ai` on `/sake/` and `/games/` | |

**Colour meaning:** vermilion = sake **and** "you are here" (nav marker). Koji (rice-straw) = games. Keep vermilion off the games theme.

Games band (scoped to .games): --g-bg #18202D  --g-raised #111824  --g-text #F5F0E6  --g-soft #B7C0CC

Every text/background pair was checked to WCAG AA (lowest ratio 5.12:1, vermilion text on the indigo contact band). **If you add a colour pair, check its contrast.** Never hard-code a colour or spacing value that should be a token.

## Typography

All fonts are **SIL OFL 1.1, self-hosted WOFF2 subsets** in `assets/fonts/` (no Google Fonts requests, no third-party calls).

- **Newsreader** (roman + italic, variable): display and editorial text. Light weights (about 340-420) for large headings.
- **"LW Sans"** (IBM Plex Sans) and **"LW Mono"** (IBM Plex Mono): body/UI and the small labels. They are **renamed** because "Plex" is a Reserved Font Name and these are modified subsets.
- **Shippori Mincho B1 Medium**: all Japanese and Chinese text, subset to *only the characters used in the HTML*. **If you add any new Japanese or Chinese character you must regenerate the subset** (`python tools/fonts.py ja`, see [../tools/README.md](../tools/README.md)), otherwise it silently falls back to a system font.
- Japanese text goes in `lang="ja"`; the Chinese name uses `lang="zh-Hant"` (same font stack). `word-break: auto-phrase` and `line-break: strict` handle Japanese wrapping.

## Motifs (use sparingly)

- **Name seal**: the ルーサー seal (vermilion tile, paper-coloured katakana), drawn from real glyph outlines. It is the logo and favicon (`assets/img/brand/seal.svg`, `assets/favicon.*`).
- **Vertical 日本酒** (`.tate`) beside sake imagery.
- **Hairline rules** and a vermilion diamond (current-page marker).
- **Rings** (`assets/img/brand/rings.svg`) concentric circles, echoing a sake cup / rice grain.
- **Low-poly wireframe** (`assets/img/brand/lowpoly.svg`, generated by `tools/lowpoly.py`) faintly behind the Games band, a nod to 3D work and Polygon Dungeon.
- **Viewport frames**: on the Games theme, screenshots get a dark bezel with koji corner marks, like a 3D viewport.
- No gradients-for-decoration, glassmorphism, emoji, stock imagery, or icon fonts.

## Layout

- 12-column grid (`.grid`, `repeat(12, minmax(0,1fr))`) from 900px up; single column below. Content width `--max` 1320px.
- Breakpoints actually used: **max-width 899px** (the main one; stacked layouts), **759px** (mobile nav, sake contents bar), **479px** (small phones). Test at **1440, about 820 (tablet), and 375**.
- Header is sticky. On the sake page the contents bar (`.chapters`) is sticky **under** the header; `html:has(.chapters)` adds scroll padding so anchors don't hide behind both. Targets inside collapsed `<details>` get `scroll-margin-top`.
- Alternating left/right project rows on `/games/` (`.project--flip`); keep the alternation when adding or reordering.

## Navigation (important: don't regress)

Luther found it **confusing when some nav links went to other pages and others scrolled the homepage**. The settled model:

- Nav = **Games · Sake · About**, plus **Contact as a small outlined button** (an action, not a place). Games and Sake are separate pages; About is the homepage `#about` section (the logo also goes home).
- The current page is marked with vermilion text and a diamond via `aria-current="page"`. It is set **in the HTML of each page** (no script). Sake is *not* permanently highlighted.
- Header/footer markup is duplicated across `index.html`, `games/index.html`, `sake/index.html`, `404.html`; **change all four**.
- The games page has a dark header variant (`.page-games .site-header`, also theme-color `#18202D`).
- Mobile: the nav collapses into a "Menu" toggle (`aria-expanded`, Escape closes), with Contact as a normal row.
- The sake page's own "on this page" bar highlights the section in view (`aria-current="location"`, small scroll-spy in `site.js`) and scrolls sideways on phones.

## Components worth knowing

- `.facts` three-up strip under the hero; `.feature`/`.band-night` sake band; `.games`, `.games-teaser` homepage band; `.contact` and `.contact--light`.
- Sake page: `.chapters`, `.glossary`, `.label-panel` (back-label style recognition panels), `.event` (class write-up with `.event__facts` and `.event-gallery`), `.note` (tasting note), `.japan` (tinted section), `.uk`, `.offer`.
- Games page: `.project` rows, `.credits` (team list), `.yt` (click-to-play video), `.earlier` (`<details>` for earlier projects), `.channel` (YouTube), `.ledger-list` (compact list rows).
- Image enlarge: native `<dialog>` (`a[data-zoom]`), works without JS as a plain link to the image.
- YouTube: thumbnail first; the `youtube-nocookie.com` iframe loads only on click. No tracking before consent.
- Reveal-on-scroll animation on headings only; fully disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility baseline (keep it)

Skip link; visible `:focus-visible` (koji on the games theme); one `h1` per page and a logical heading order; real `alt` text on content images (decorative images `alt=""`); `aria-current`; `aria-expanded` on the toggle; native `<dialog>`; reduced-motion support; `lang` attributes for Japanese/Chinese; print styles hide navigation. All pages must work at 375px with **no horizontal overflow** (check `document.documentElement.scrollWidth === innerWidth`).

## Avoid the AI look

Luther explicitly asked for these to be removed (Nov 2026). **Don't reintroduce them:**

1. **Numbered section labels** ("01 / 02 / 03", numbered contents bars, "01 ——— Games").
2. **Slogan headlines** ("Sake, studied closely and shared generously", "Four ingredients, endless nuance", "Let's talk sake", "Sake worth remembering").
3. **An italic accent word** inside a big headline.
4. **Full stops at the end of titles.** No headline, card title or heading ends with a full stop (body text is normal).
5. **Repeating the same credentials** (WSET + Dassai) on every section. Say it properly once or twice.
6. **Small all-caps labels above headings that repeat the heading**, and decorative labels ("QUALIFICATION", "AWARD"). Keep labels only when they carry information: dates, "Upcoming", the class facts (2 hours, 12 guests, 8 sakes).
7. **Arrows on every link.** Arrows only on main buttons (→) and links that leave the site (↗).
8. **Placeholder boxes** ("Photograph to follow", "No public events scheduled"). Don't ship empty states; add the section when there is content.
9. **Filler and stock phrases**: "I'm always happy to hear from people", "a joy to share", "My aim is simple", lists of three used for rhythm, dash-heavy sentences.

What reads as human, and should stay: specific, slightly unexpected details in his own voice (the "bacon and almonds" tasting note; wine experts asking about fining and shubo; "a duel for their life, literally"; the long-legged bee), and opinions marked as his ("In my opinion, it's also the best drink to pair with any food").

## Things deliberately **not** done

No framework, build step, CDN, icon font, or web-font service. No cookie banner (nothing needs one: GoatCounter is cookie-free and YouTube loads only on click). No dark-mode toggle (the page is a considered paper design; sections provide the dark moments). No stock or AI-generated imagery, ever.
